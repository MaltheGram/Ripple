# Sign in to GitLab

Ripple works as **you**: comments, drafts and approvals are made with your GitLab account.

1. Click **Sign in to GitLab**.
2. Your browser opens GitLab. Approve Ripple.
3. You're sent back to VS Code. If VS Code asks whether Ripple may use the account, allow it.

Your token is stored in the operating system's keychain, never in files or settings.
