# Pick a merge request

Open the **Ripple** view in the activity bar. The **Merge Requests** list shows:

- **Review requested**: MRs waiting for your review
- **Assigned to me** and **Created by me**
- **All open in your group**, grouped by project

Click one. The first time for a project, Ripple downloads it into its own checkout (`~/.ripple`), so your own clones are never touched. Later MRs only fetch what changed.
